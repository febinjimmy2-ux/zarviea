ZARVIEA GIFT STUDIO — WEBSITE FILES

HOW TO OPEN
1. Extract zarviea_complete_website.zip.
2. Open the extracted zarviea_complete folder in Visual Studio Code.
3. Open index.html in your browser, or use the Live Server extension.
4. The images are already inside the images folder.

PAGES
- index.html       Home
- shop.html        Product collection
- customize.html   Custom hamper builder and price estimate
- cart.html        Shopping bag
- reviews.html     Feedback form
- about.html       Our story
- contact.html     Contact form

EDIT PRICES
Open script.js and edit the price numbers in the products array.
Custom hamper extras and size prices are in customize.html (the checkbox values and size option values).

WHATSAPP ORDERS
The WhatsApp number is intentionally left blank in script.js (WA_NUMBER).
Only add an approved BUSINESS WhatsApp number with permission from a parent/guardian.
Use digits only with country code, for example 91XXXXXXXXXX.
Do not put a personal number online without permission.

REVIEWS
The review form saves entries in the same browser using localStorage. This is only a local preview and does NOT send reviews to you or collect them from other visitors. For public customer reviews, connect a trusted form service or a backend before launch.

IMPORTANT BEFORE PUBLISHING
- The prices and products are sample content; confirm real prices and availability.
- Add your business contact details and delivery/return policy.
- Check image rights and make sure product photos accurately represent items you sell.
- This is a front-end demo; it does not process online payments or store orders on a server.


WHATSAPP CART CHECKOUT UPDATE
- Customers can add multiple products and quantities to the cart.
- At checkout they enter their name, contact number and delivery address.
- Buy via WhatsApp prepares one message containing all items, quantities and the total.
- The customer must tap Send in WhatsApp. The website cannot detect whether WhatsApp sent the message, so it must not claim the order is confirmed.
- The seller should reply to confirm the order. This demo does not process payments or send automatic SMS/email receipts.
- To enable ordering, add an approved business WhatsApp number (country code + number, digits only) to WA_NUMBER in script.js. Keep personal contact details private; use a business contact approved by a parent/guardian.
